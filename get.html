Get the app

Run the Weights works best from your Home Screen. Your plan and sets stay on your phone, in the place you open the app from.

iPhone

  1. Open runtheweights.vercel.app in Safari.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Run the Weights from your Home Screen and log from there.

Do this before you log anything. Safari and the Home Screen app do not share data.

Android

  1. Open runtheweights.vercel.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu with three dots, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Run the Weights from your Home Screen.

Back up my data

  1. In the app, tap the gear at the top to open Settings.
  2. Tap Backup. A file saves to your phone.
  3. Keep it somewhere safe, such as Files or iCloud Drive.

Restore

  1. Open the app where you want your data, for example from your Home Screen.
  2. Tap the gear, then Restore.
  3. Pick your backup file. Your plan, sets and weights come back.

Updates

Updates install on their own. The newest version loads the next time you open the app.

Something wrong?

Email and say what you saw. A screenshot helps.
