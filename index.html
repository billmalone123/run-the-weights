    Why it matters

    STRONG
    FEELS GOOD

    Getting stronger is not about how you look to anyone else. It shows up in how you feel, how you sleep and how much you can handle in a day.

    01
    Mental Edge
    Confidence
    Is Not Born.
    It's Built.

    Every rep you finish when you wanted to stop is a deposit into your self-belief. Finish this program and you will walk into rooms differently. Not because you told yourself to. Because your body knows what you put into it.

    Feeling strong changes how you carry yourself, at work, with friends and everywhere in between. That ease comes from the work you do in here.

    02
    More Energy
    More Left
    For The Rest
    Of Your Day.

    Regular strength work helps you sleep better, think clearer and handle long days. You feel it at your desk, on a night out and the morning after.

    You cannot add hours to the day. You can have more energy for them. Two or three sessions a week is enough to feel it.

    03
    Athletic Output
    Strong Is
    Useful.

    Mobility work is built into every session. You will feel strong and move well in everyday life. Carry the groceries in one trip, take the stairs, join the game.

    You do not need a perfect week. Show up when you can and the strength adds up.

    Every lift runs on double progression: two sets, the last one taken close to your limit. When your best set reaches the top of the rep range, the app suggests the next weight: 5 lbs for upper body, 10 lbs for lower body. Until then, aim for one more rep than last time. Reps dipping after a weight increase is normal. That is a step up, not a setback. The cards below show every lift you log: this week against last, the trend over four sessions, your total gain, and how often your heaviest set reached failure.
    Show up when you can. Pick up where you left off.
    The Code
    01Hard lifting and hard running never share a day. One of them always gives.
    02Hip mobility every leg day. It's not just your squat. It's your stride.
    03Rest fully between heavy sets and after hard runs. Recovery is part of the work.
    04Every fourth week, ease off, lifting and running both. Rest is how you keep going.
    05Log every set. If you're not tracking, you're not progressing.
    06Built around your days and your equipment. Adjust the plan when life changes.

    CHEST + BACK
    Horizontal push and pull. Balanced pressing and rowing volume.
    Day 1 ~1:20
    Warm-Up. Complete Before First Work Set
    1
    5 min incline walk or row machine
    Raise core temp and get blood to the chest and back before loading.
    2
    Band pull-aparts — 2×20
    Wake up the rear delts and external rotators before any pressing.
    3
    Shoulder CARs — 10 slow circles each arm
    Opens the shoulder joint through full range before loading. Non-negotiable on chest day.
    4
    Bench ramp-up: bar×10, 50%×5, 65%×3
    Never jump straight to work sets. Progressive load to working weight.
